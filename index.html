❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Which cosmetic & skincare brands do you use?▼
We use 100% authentic, sealed professional products including O3+, MAC, Kryolan, L'Oreal Professionnel, Huda Beauty, Lotus, OPI, and Rica.
2. Can I book bridal or party makeup for my home or wedding venue?▼
Yes! In the booking form, simply choose "At Wedding Venue / Home" under Service Location Preference.
3. Can clients from Birganj, Nepal book online?▼
Absolutely! Our booking form supports both India (🇮🇳 +91) and Nepal (🇳🇵 +977) mobile numbers.
4. How can I reschedule my appointment date or time?▼
Go to the Book & Track tab, view your recent booking or enter your Booking ID, and tap the 📅 Reschedule button to pick a new date and time.
